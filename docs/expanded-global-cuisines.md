# Expanded global cuisines

The 2026-09-13 expansion adds 30 recognizable dishes: five each from a broad East/Southeast Asian group, Indian, American, Italian, Spanish, and Mexican cooking. Selection favors dishes that are widely recognized, practical in a home kitchen, and different from recipes already in SnapChef.

Food Network’s Indian and Mexican home-cooking collections, Airbnb’s *Kitchens of Asia* collection, Lidia Bastianich’s Italian and Italian-American materials, and general cuisine indexes were used to cross-check popular dish names, origins, and staple ingredients. SnapChef’s descriptions, ingredient lists, and concise core methods were written specifically for the application; source recipe text was not copied.

Every addition uses the existing recipe schema, so it participates in search, Global and dietary categories, time and difficulty filters, pantry matching, serving adjustment, shopping lists, favorites, history, and Cooking Mode.
