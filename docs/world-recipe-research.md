# World recipe catalog research

Updated 2026-09-13. This expansion adds a curated set of recognizable, home-cookable dishes from Asia, the Middle East, Africa, Europe, and the Americas. It complements SnapChef's Filipino-first collection; it is not intended as a definitive ranking or a complete account of any cuisine.

Selection was cross-checked against:

- Good Food's most-visited community recipes, which highlights enduring home-cooking choices such as spaghetti carbonara and chili: <https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/howto/guide/most-popular-recipes-on-good-food>
- SAVEUR's international collection of 150 classic recipes, spanning dishes such as falafel, borscht, samosas, and red-cooked eggplant: <https://www.saveur.com/gallery2/150-Classic-Recipes/>
- TasteAtlas's audience-rated world dish and traditional recipe catalogs, used as a discovery source rather than a final authority: <https://www.tasteatlas.com/best-rated-dishes-in-the-world/> and <https://www.tasteatlas.com/best-recipes-in-the-world>
- SAVEUR's global curry collection, which documents the category's many regional home-cooking traditions: <https://www.saveur.com/global-curries/>

All descriptions, ingredient lists, and directions were written specifically for SnapChef. They are concise ingredient-matching guides, not reproductions of source recipes. Names identify broadly familiar versions; regional and household variations are expected. Users should follow standard food-safety practices, especially when cooking meat, eggs, and shellfish.
